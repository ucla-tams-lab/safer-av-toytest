Terminal-State-Aware Long-Horizon Sim-to-Real Evaluation: toy study

A fixed goal-conditioned policy visits K goals in a PointMaze maze; the "real" system is the simulator with weaker force transmission and braking. From n short real–simulation paired transitions (plus simulated transitions), each method estimates the real cost of 536 test goal sequences, separately for five costs (Safety, Exposure, TTC, Path, Time). All numbers are mean absolute errors averaged over 9 runs (3 data seeds × 3 model seeds). Setting, metrics and method details: README.md and METHODOLOGY.md.

Key results (mean over 9 runs)
  • The proposed direct estimator beats every baseline. For K = 2, 4 and 8 its error is below every baseline for all five costs and every n. Against target-only, the strongest baseline (the same backward Bellman learning on real data only), it reduces the K=8 error by 11–61% across costs and n.
  • Correcting only segment outcomes is not enough. Segment-wise outcome correction (SCAPE-style) has 10–30× the error of target-only on Safety and Exposure, because it ignores the terminal-state mismatch.
  • The Bellman decomposition is needed. Learning long-sequence costs directly from the same real rollouts (B1, B2) has 2.9–16× the error of the direct estimator.
  • The best proposed estimator depends on the cost: Safety: Residual direct, Sim-backed; Exposure: Direct, Sim-backed; TTC: Direct, Residual direct; Path: Residual direct; Time: Residual direct. The simulation-backed estimator is worse than target-only on Path, Time for most n.
  • At K = 1 no correction is needed. Test sequences start at rest, and one segment from rest is almost the same in real and simulation; raw simulation is the most accurate at K = 1 (Safety, Exposure, Path).

Error by sequence length K

Errors grow with K for every method, since costs accumulate over more segments and terminal-state mismatches carry over to the remaining goals. At K = 1 raw simulation is already accurate; from K = 2 on, the direct and residual estimators are below target-only and raw simulation.

Limitations

  • Sim2Sim study (real = simulation with changed dynamics) in one maze with 9 fixed goals; not tested on a physical system or new layouts.
  • The 75% share of paired targets in the training rows of Wθ was chosen by looking at test results (a small sweep).
  • The residual variant needs multi-segment simulation rollouts from arbitrary states during training.
